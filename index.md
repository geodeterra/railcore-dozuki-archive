# RailCore build guides (unofficial archive)

This is an unofficial community mirror of <https://railcore.dozuki.com/>, the Dozuki site that hosts the RailCore® II and RailCore® Mini Kit build guides. It was captured on 2026-11-16 because the Dozuki site may be shut down. Each page links back to its original URL.

RailCore® is a family of CoreXY RepRap 3D printers designed by J. Steve White, Tony Akens and Ben Withem (<https://railcore.org/>). The guides were written on railcore.dozuki.com by Tony Akens and Ben Withem; comments are credited to their authors on each page.
